## How to read this packet

This packet is for review. It has one section per pathway and a verification section at the end.

Tier definitions. Copied from the design spec (section 6.2), `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-21-pathway-knowledge-graph-design.md`:

- **T1a:** the fungal enzyme assayed, or its product identified after heterologous expression.
- **T1b:** gene deletion or complementation with intermediate analysis in the fungus.
- **T1c:** deletion with colour phenotype only.
- **T2:** reaction shown in another organism for the same enzyme family.
- **T3:** inferred from family or orthology.
- **T4:** hypothesis, no direct evidence.
A tier is bound to the named organism and protein. Orthologues in other species are T3 until a paper shows the reaction. A path with any T4 step is not reported as possible. The capability score reports the lowest tier and the lowest identity to the T1 enzyme along the path. No identity threshold is fixed in this version; the builder proposes one from the decoy data.

Labels used in the curator's notes (copied from `UNVERIFIED.md`):

Labels: MEASURED = read from a database record or abstract on 2026-11-21; REVIEWED = stated by the pathway review (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-21-pathway-knowledge-graph-REVIEW_PATHWAYS.md`), not re-checked; PROPOSED = curator's choice; UNVERIFIED = not checked.

Reading the route tables:

- `min tier` is the weakest tier along the best path.
- The links of that path can belong to different organisms. `organisms` lists them.
- When `single_organism` is False, the tier does not hold for any one organism. Read it only together with the organism list.
- `n_equal_paths` counts organism lists with the best tier and the fewest organisms. It does not count routes through the reactions.
- A route with no product row cannot be followed by the tables. The reason is in the decision items of the pathway.

Decision numbers: `DC` carotenoid, `DD` melanin_dhn, `DL` melanin_ldopa, `DM` mycosporine. The letter is followed by the position of the item in `UNVERIFIED.md`.
