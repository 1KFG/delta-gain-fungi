Rendered reports from the DeltaGain analyses. Each page is generated from a REPORT.md that lives beside the code that produced it, in analysis/ — the page and the source therefore cannot drift.

These are working analyses. Every result is computational; nothing here has been expressed, assayed or otherwise tested at the bench, and each report states its own limitations.

Available

Delta Gain: findings synthesis

The novelty-yield measurement this project is named for: how much new protein-level information newly annotated fungal genomes add beyond UniProt and RefSeq. Whole-proteome funnel, what the surviving clusters are, family-level diversity against an order-matched null, and species accumulation across 23,567 genomes. Reproduced verbatim from the pipeline repository at a pinned commit.

One result on it has since been qualified — see the box at the top of the page, and its companion below.

Source: 1KFG/delta-gain @ 91eb5fe

The enriched-Pfam signal is mostly Y1000+ deposition

A companion test to the funnel’s largest family-level effect. 1,033 genomes from two UW-Madison Y1000+ BioProjects — 5.8 % of the new side — carry about three quarters of the enriched families’ novelty, because their proteins went to Figshare rather than to the protein databases the baseline is built from. Counting them as known inverts the effect. The random-Pfam controls barely move, which is what makes it a confound rather than a correction to everything.

Source: analysis/delta_gain_funnel/

Unspecific peroxygenases across 15,137 fungal genomes

A census of 97,520 PF01328 proteins, phylogenetic placement onto a 187-leaf reference tree, and a candidate list for expression. The report is as specific about what the model cannot do as about what it can: finding active-site residues is validated 95/95, ranking clade-V-like pockets works for ordering candidates, and predicting whether an arbitrary protein is an active peroxygenase is not reliable — out-of-clade AUC 0.556 on the 19 characterized enzymes the field has. It also reports four attempts to lift that ceiling that failed their own tests, and two errors found in the underlying genome database.

Source: analysis/enzymes_UPO/
