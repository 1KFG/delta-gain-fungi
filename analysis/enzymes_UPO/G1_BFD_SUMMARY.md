## 6. Glu180 and the catalytic-core filter

The 2025 paper reports Glu180 (HspUPO numbering) in all 19 characterized UPOs. Placement shows that its absence is **lineage-structured**, so it cannot be read as a per-protein 'not a UPO' test without knowing the lineage.

**Clade I by phylum** (Clade I = Zamocky Family II, long):

| phylum | n | Glu180 | most common residues at 180 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ascomycota | 33783 | 1.5 % | A 28408, S 2244, T 1124, - 700 |
| Basidiomycota | 7895 | 66.9 % | E 5280, A 1341, - 591, T 151 |
| Chytridiomycota | 1 | 0.0 % | - 1 |

The earlier report read the 16.8 % Glu180 rate of old Clade I calls as the low-identity bin absorbing non-UPOs. Placement puts 99.8 % of those proteins inside Family II, and the non-Glu residue (mostly Ala) is concentrated in **ascomycete long UPOs**, matching all 13 curated ascomycete Family II sequences in Zamocky et al. 2022 (A/S/T at this position). The five characterized Clade I enzymes are basidiomycete and carry Glu. So the low rate is a property of ascomycete long UPOs, not evidence of contamination. **Open:** whether they lack the acid-base Glu or carry it at a position that does not align to HspUPO 180 (test against a structure-based alignment of an ascomycete long UPO).

**Basidiomycetous 1st** (an early lineage outside Clades I-V) lacks Glu180 almost entirely: 11/6378 carry Glu; all 34 curated members carry Asn or Ser.
